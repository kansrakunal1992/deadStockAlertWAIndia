Draft Register Loop Note — Stage 3: Life events are event-triggered; their payroll impact is validated during the draft register cycle. Both Draft 1 and Draft 2 are currently manual — Excel email to AI Payroll HRSS. Risk is elevated for Stage 3 because each life event (TMU, Suspension, Absconding) involves a unique pay computation — errors in draft are harder to catch without a structured validation tool. · Bank File (PS → Bank SFTP) is semi-automated: PS generates, manual release required.
Stage 3 Key Notes (Component View):  ·  Only semi-auto (L4 Promotions): 3 comps (1.6%) — SF Comp Planning system; auditable; SF EC → PS auto-sync after upload — lowest risk.  ·  All other 8 inputs = Manual: 22 comps (12%) — all Excel-driven with no audit trail.  ·  CAE → SF automation (L1, L3): Technically exists but HR re-validates via Excel before payroll action — re-validation loop is a Phase 1 automation target.  ·  Absconding (L9): Triggers Full & Final (FnF) process → Channel F — recovery computation is complex and error-prone in manual state.  ·  Draft Register Validation (×2): Common to all PS rows; manual Excel email loop — especially risky for life events given unique computation per event.
