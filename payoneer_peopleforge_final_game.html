Welcome to Project Orbit

You will play through eight short scenes. Choose what to do, see what happens, learn one useful skill, and then try that skill in a normal work situation.

  • Understand: Do you know the real problem?
  • Trust: Do people trust your decisions?
  • Moving: Is the work moving forward?
  • Use: Will people use the solution?
  • Value: Can you show that it helped?

Characters will react to your choices. Some choices help now but create problems later.
